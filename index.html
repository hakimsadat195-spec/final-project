About TravelBlooom

We believe every journey tells a story. Since 2015, we've helped millions of travelers discover their perfect destinations — crafting personalized experiences that turn dreams into unforgettable memories.

Our mission and values

Our mission

Connecting travelers with extraordinary destinations through personalized, data-driven recommendations they can trust.

Our values

Authenticity, inclusivity, sustainability, and a deep respect for the cultures and communities we visit.

Our goals

Empower 50 million travelers by 2028 with curated, responsible travel experiences across 190+ countries.

Our purpose

To make the world feel smaller, more accessible, and more wondrous for every type of traveler.

Our story

2015
Founded in Amsterdam
Sofia Lin and Marco Ricci launch TravelBlooom from a small apartment with a mission to democratize travel discovery.
2017
First million users
We hit 1 million registered travelers and launch our AI-powered recommendations engine, transforming how users discover destinations.
2019
Global expansion
Opened offices in Singapore and São Paulo. Launched multilingual support for 22 languages and partnered with 3,000+ local guides.
2021
Sustainability pledge
Committed to carbon-neutral operations and launched the "Travel Responsibly" certification program for eco-friendly destinations.
2024
30 million travelers served
Surpassed 30 million users worldwide. Recognized as one of Time Magazine's most influential travel platforms.
